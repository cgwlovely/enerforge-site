About

Heliovulcan is an independent research practice in Australian industry, energy and emissions

Published research is self-directed and is not presented on behalf of a client. Commissioned work is described separately and, where confidential, by capability rather than by client name.

What Heliovulcan does

Energy and emissions analysis, sector reconciliation, project screening

Research

Independent, self-directed research

  • National emissions inventory
  • Facility-level energy and emissions modelling
  • Mining and remote energy
  • Industrial decarbonisation
  • Government policy and energy-transition analysis
  • Early-stage project and financeability screening
Commissioned work

Commercial work is described by capability, not by client name

Most commissioned work is done under confidentiality, so it is described by field rather than by client or project. Public examples show selected methods and non-confidential outputs; source architecture, internal scoring and opportunity assessments stay internal. The work identifies the important gaps before detailed engineering or formal due diligence begins and does not replace either.

Who the work is for

The decisions it supports

The work helps different users answer different questions. It may support a decision to continue, investigate further, redesign or stop.

WhoDecision supported
Asset owner or operator Establish the current energy, emissions and control position before considering any change to it
Project developer Identify host constraints, missing evidence, and whether a concept merits further work at all
Investor, lender or adviser Screen asset or project exposure, and determine what requires formal diligence
Government or industry body Understand facility, sector or portfolio patterns from reconciled public evidence

Evidence

How the work is disciplined

The work starts with the record, not an estimate. Facility accounts are reconstructed from regulatory filings, licences and company disclosures, then checked against independently assembled sector and national data. Calculated figures, unresolved questions and evidentiary limits are identified in the published work. See the research and evidence method →

Have a question worth checking?

Send a short, non-confidential description of the facility, project, sector or policy question. An initial conversation is usually enough to tell whether public information can help. Some questions lead to published research; others become commissioned work.

What an answer looks like

What you get back

  • What public data establishes, with a grade on each figure
  • What it cannot establish
  • The constraints and open questions that bear on the decision
  • Data to request from the owner, where needed
  • A next step, which can be to stop

Outside the scope. EPC engineering design, legal, tax or financial advice, an investment recommendation, lender due diligence, or a certified bankable feasibility study.

Useful context for a site question

  • Site type and the decision you are facing
  • Annual electricity use or diesel consumption, approximate
  • Current energy or fuel cost and rough load pattern

None of it is required to start; a research, sector or policy question needs none of it.
