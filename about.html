About

An independent research and analysis practice focused on Australian industry, energy and emissions

We use public records and agreed project information to analyse industrial facilities, sectors and proposed energy projects. The work identifies the important gaps before detailed engineering or formal due diligence begins. It does not replace engineering, environmental studies, legal advice or formal due diligence.

What Heliovulcan does

Energy and emissions analysis, sector reconciliation, project screening

Relevant experience

Where the work comes from

  • National emissions inventory
  • Facility-level energy and emissions modelling
  • Mining and remote energy
  • Industrial decarbonisation
  • Government policy and energy-transition analysis
  • Early-stage project and financeability screening
How we describe it

By field, not by client

Most of this work was done under confidentiality, so it is described by field rather than by client or project name. Outputs include research, asset profiles, project screens, the F1–F4 Pre-DD review and sector analysis.

Who the work is for

The decisions it supports

The work helps different users answer different questions. It may support a decision to continue, investigate further, redesign or stop.

WhoDecision supported
Asset owner or operator Establish the current energy, emissions and control position before considering any change to it
Project developer Identify host constraints, missing evidence, and whether a concept merits further work at all
Investor, lender or adviser Screen asset or project exposure, and determine what requires formal diligence
Government or industry body Understand facility, sector or portfolio patterns from reconciled public evidence

Evidence principles

Evidence before estimate

01

Evidence before estimate

What can be shown from the record, separated from what is inferred and what is simply missing.

02

Reconciliation against something built differently

Two figures agreeing is only evidence when their inputs do not overlap.

03

Who can actually decide

Ownership, operator and contract control frequently sit somewhere other than where they appear to.

04

Negative results are results

A market that is smaller than it looks is a finding, and it is published like any other.

05

Label every figure

Regulatory filing, licence record, company disclosure or calculated; not found is recorded as not found, never as zero.

06

Say where it stops

What public sources cannot give goes on the owner-data request, not into an estimate.

What an answer looks like, and what it is not

What you get back. What public data establishes, with a grade on every figure; what it cannot establish, listed rather than guessed; the constraints and open questions that bear on the decision; a list of data to request from the owner where one is needed; a clear next step, which can be to stop.

What it is not. EPC engineering design, legal, tax or financial advice, an investment recommendation, lender due diligence, or a certified bankable feasibility study.

Useful context for a site question. Site type; annual electricity use or diesel consumption (approximate is fine); current energy or fuel cost; rough load pattern; the decision you are facing. None of it is required to start, and a research, sector or policy question needs none of it.
