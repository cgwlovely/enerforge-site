Method

Data sources

Every input is listed with its source. Anything that could not be sourced from public material is identified as such. Errors identified by readers are corrected and each correction is published with its date.

Facility-level calculation

A facility's published baseline is scaled by the ratio of ERC values year by year (Rule s 11), held at 100,000 t where it would fall below (s 10(1)), and compared with covered emissions moved by the commodity production factor. Facilities with a trade-exposed determination follow the ERC path the Regulator has published. Credits are measured against the baseline before the minimum is applied (s 57). The full derivation is under How this is calculated on the Pathway and Policy pages.

Policy scenarios

The 2035 target, the post-2030 decline rate, the treatment of trade-exposed determinations after expiry, the production outlook, closures, new entrants and additional abatement are settings. Current policy on the starting-point assumptions is the reference case; the decomposition on the Policy page attributes any difference to one setting at a time.

Data dictionary. erc_fy2024_25 is the emissions reduction contribution applied in the year (0.902 default; slower values for facilities with a trade-exposed determination). baseline_t and covered_emissions_t are the Regulator’s published values in tonnes CO₂-e; a baseline of 100,000 may be the statutory minimum rather than the calculated value. commodity_group is Heliovulcan’s assignment for the production factor. Limitations

  • The model is based on the FY2024-25 register. A facility that has not yet reported is not represented except through the new-entrant setting.
  • Facility baselines are the published values scaled by the change in the ERC. They are not re-derived from each facility's production and intensity, so a facility whose production changes materially will diverge from the modelled path until the next register is published.
  • Production assumptions are national commodity paths applied to every facility in that commodity. An individual facility may move in the opposite direction.
  • The paths for facilities with a trade-exposed determination are those the Regulator has published. The determinations expire, and their treatment after expiry is a policy setting rather than an input.
  • The results are calculations on stated assumptions, not a compliance determination or a forecast.
