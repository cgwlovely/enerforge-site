Updates

What has changed, including the figures we have had to correct

This page lists every published change to the research, the datasets and the tools, newest first. 2 of the 7 entries below are corrections to figures that had already been published. Those are the entries most worth reading.

Get these by email Subscribe by feed →

  • 10 October 2026Interactive tool

    CBAM charge explorer: move the certificate price and see what it costs by origin

    The explorer puts the EU carbon border charge on one screen for 198 origin countries. Moving the certificate price re-prices every origin, so a reader can see which exposures are sensitive to the price and which are driven by tonnage.

  • 10 October 2026Dataset

    The CBAM country layer is published as data, with what is held back stated on the page

    Three files cover the country layer: exposure for 198 origins, the verification comparison for 11 origins, and the source register. Facility-level cross-source identifiers, the matching rules and the per-plant calibrated intensities are not published, and the methods page says so.

  • 4 October 2026Research report

    The CBAM verification gap: what an exporter pays at default values against what it pays on a verified figure

    For 11 origins the charge can be compared at EU default values against the published country average intensity. The report sets out the method and the source register alongside the result.

  • 27 September 2026Correction

    Research coverage was stated as 21 facility profiles across 3 sectors. It is 49 across 16

    The coverage page carried a count that had not been updated as profiles were completed. The page now reports 49 profiles across 16 sectors and is rebuilt from the profile record rather than written by hand.

  • 27 September 2026Interactive tool

    Critical minerals: power and grid readiness

    A screen over Australian critical-mineral projects covering the power arrangement each one has, whether a station is owned by the miner or a contractor, and how far the site sits from the network.

  • 27 September 2026Interactive tool

    The Safeguard Atlas is rebuilt as a single page, laid out like a submission

    The Atlas now reads in one pass: the rule, the arithmetic, one setting changed at a time, and the register-change comparison between FY2023-24 and FY2024-25. The 209 register rows and the model inputs are available to registered readers as a packaged file.

  • 16 September 2026Correction

    Baseline-floor study: two corrections dated 16 September 2026

    A fact check against the FY2024-25 and FY2023-24 registers and the Rule text changed two figures. The median baseline of the floored cohort is 127,011 t CO2-e, not 127,780 t. The difference between the 24-facility and 23-facility counts is not explained by deemed surrenders, and the earlier text said it was.

Entries run from 16 September 2026 to 10 October 2026. Changes to navigation, layout and internal tooling are not listed here, because they do not change what a reader can rely on. A correction is listed whether or not it favours our earlier conclusion.
