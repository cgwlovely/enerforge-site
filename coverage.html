Facility-level profiles

A profile establishes what a single operation consumes, burns, emits, produces and pays — reconciled against national accounts, with every figure carrying an evidence grade and the unobtainable items named rather than estimated.

What "end to end" means

For aluminium smelting and alumina refining, every facility in the country is profiled. That matters because it is the only condition under which a national reconciliation can be checked: sum the facilities, compare to the published national figure, and the difference is either explained or it is a finding. Gold is explicitly not in that position — 11 profiles out of a much larger sector, and treated as a sample throughout.

Production

Sector output

Facility-by-facility production summed and compared to published national totals. Aluminium closed to within a fraction of a per cent; alumina to a few per cent, with the residual named.

Energy

Generation and fuel

National electricity generation reconciled against the register of designated generating facilities, with the residual decomposed by fuel and by state rather than left as a gap.

Emissions

Carbon and pollutants

Facility carbon accounts cross-checked against independent pollutant inventories — two databases with no shared inputs, which is the only way a check is a check.

Market

Sector-level demand

Bottom-up site analysis reconciled against national energy statistics to establish a ceiling, not just a list. Published example: the diesel replacement market.

A reconciliation that fails is worth more than one that passes. Twice, the failure was in our own numbers.

Limits

What this coverage does not mean

It is not a database you can query

These are analytical products built for specific questions. There is no subscription product, no search interface, and no claim of live currency across the whole set.

Screening is not sizing

A screening layer establishes exposure and rank order. It never establishes a system size, a connection outcome or a price. Those need owner data.

Grades differ within a profile

Inside one profile, some figures are drawn directly from regulatory filings and some are derived. They are labelled differently, and derived values are not used to carry a headline finding on their own.

Some questions are closed as unobtainable

Interval load curves, unit run hours, contract price and volume terms, and standby strategy are not in the public record. Where that is the answer, it is stated as the answer.

Heliovulcan is an independent industrial intelligence practice. It reconstructs Australian industrial assets and energy projects from public records and agreed inputs — energy, emissions, spatial, ownership and commercial context — reconciles them against independently built accounts, and grades the evidence behind every figure.
