Critical minerals · power and grid readiness · data checked 27 September 2026

Every Australian critical-minerals project, ranked by when it is next likely to spend money on power

This map covers 538 Australian critical-mineral sites — 117 mines and 421 known deposits not currently classified as mines. They are plotted here against 1,805 mapped substations, because distance to the grid is the one commercial question that can be answered for every site. Each row says what its evidence does not settle.

Start here: use the filters to narrow the list. Then select a map point or table row to see the evidence for one site. Results are ordered by the energy-opportunity signal, and within each group the sites farthest from a mapped substation come first. This is a research screen: it does not show whether a project will proceed, whether grid capacity is available, or what a connection would cost.
