Website policyEffective 10 October 2026

Privacy

Heliovulcan receives personal information in two ways: when you contact the business by email, and when you register on this site for the datasets and the update notices. This notice explains what is collected in each case, where it is held and how to have it removed.

Information you choose to provide

The site has one form, the registration form at /subscribe.html, and it is covered in its own section below. If you use an email link instead, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, organisation, project description and high-level energy information.

Please do not send confidential engineering files, personal information about other people, commercially sensitive data or credentials with an initial enquiry. If a scoped engagement proceeds, we will request and agree any additional information separately.

How enquiry information is used

Information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry, assess whether Heliovulcan can help with the project, prepare a proposed scope, maintain a record of the discussion and meet legal or professional obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use enquiry information for unrelated advertising.

Website hosting and technical records

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. The site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or operate its own visitor analytics. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary technical records such as IP address, request time, browser information and requested URL for security and service operation under their own policies.

Registration and the update notices

Registering is optional and free, and nothing on the site is withheld from you for declining. The form asks for an email address, which is required, and for a name, an organisation, a role and the subjects you want notices about, all of which are optional. If you arrive from a particular file, the name of that file is recorded with your registration so we know what you came for.

That information is held in our mailing-list account with MailerLite, which processes it on our instructions and under its own terms. It is used for two purposes only: sending you the files and the links to them, and telling you when the research changes. We do not sell the list, we do not rent it, and we do not pass it to any other party.

You confirm your address before anything is sent, every message carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your record from the list. The same notices are published at /updates.html and as a feed at /updates.xml, neither of which requires an email address or any registration.

Registration is not a commercial engagement, and it creates no obligation on either side.

Files sent on registration

The packaged datasets and the submissions are delivered as links to a shared folder rather than as attachments. A link sent to you can be opened by anyone you forward it to, so treat it as you would any shared link. The folder provider processes access under its own terms.

Email and third-party services

Website email links open your chosen email service and messages are processed by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers. Linked documents may be stored and delivered by third-party hosting providers. Those providers apply their own privacy and security terms.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we reasonably need it for the discussion, a resulting engagement, record-keeping or legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect enquiry information, but no email or internet transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to delete it unless we must retain it for legal or record-keeping purposes. Send the request to the same email address used for website enquiries.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the website adds analytics, cookies or other services. We will revise the effective date above when we make significant changes. The change made on 10 October 2026 was the addition of the registration form and the mailing list it writes to.

Initial enquiries

For an initial enquiry, send only non-confidential, high-level information. Do not send detailed engineering files, personal information or commercially sensitive material until we have agreed how it will be handled.
