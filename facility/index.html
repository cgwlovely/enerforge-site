Safeguard facilities by state

The Clean Energy Regulator's FY2024-25 Safeguard register contains 209 facility rows with 132.8 Mt CO₂-e of covered emissions. There is one page for each row. Where a facility changed operator during the year, both operators reported it and the register contains two rows under one name. Each page gives the facility's covered emissions, its baseline, the composition of its emissions by gas, the change from the previous register, and its modelled baseline path to FY2034-35.

Facilities are listed by state and then by covered emissions. Safeguard Atlas provides the searchable table and the policy settings across all facilities. The 209 rows carry 208 distinct facility names; the difference is a mid-year change of operator at one facility.
