Research approach

Analytical research notes, built from the public record

Not formal academic publications and not investment advice. They are written to help owners, developers, investors and policy readers think more clearly about decisions that turn on evidence. Every figure carries its evidence grade; unresolved questions are named rather than filled in. Several pieces reach a negative conclusion, that a market is smaller than it looks or that a route does not exist, and those are published for the same reason as the rest.

Themes

What the research keeps coming back to

01

Evidence before estimate

What can be shown from the record, separated from what is inferred and what is simply missing.

02

Revenue that survives a contract

How customer savings become, or fail to become, stable, bankable revenue.

03

Reconciliation against something built differently

Two figures agreeing is only evidence when their inputs do not overlap.

04

Who can actually decide

Ownership, operator and contract control frequently sit somewhere other than where they appear to.

05

Weak-grid and diesel sites

The practical realities of operations where diesel is backup, peak support and reliability insurance at once.

06

Negative results are results

A market that is smaller than it looks is a finding, and it is published like any other.

Where research has been submitted to a public consultation, it is recorded on the consultation record. The methods behind the facility-level work are on the methods page.
