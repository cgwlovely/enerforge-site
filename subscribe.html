Registration

Registration is free. It exists so we know who relies on this work

There is no payment, no tier and no trial. An email address gets you the packaged datasets and the submissions, and a short notice whenever the research changes. We ask for an organisation and a role because knowing who uses a figure changes how we maintain it.

What stays open whether or not you register

Every page of analysis stays open, and so does the data each interactive tool works from. The tools recalculate in your browser, which means they carry their figures with them. For 3 of the datasets above the same fields can therefore be read without registering, and the submissions are the files this list actually withholds. We would rather say that here than let a reader find it and wonder what else the page was quiet about.

What registration adds for those 3 datasets is the packaged file, the column definitions and the change notices — not access to the numbers.

Why the notices are the part that matters

We correct figures after publication. Of the 7 entries on the updates page, 2 are corrections to numbers that had already been published and had already been read. A correction is listed whether or not it favours our earlier conclusion.

If you have used one of these datasets in your own work, the notice telling you a figure moved is worth more than the original download. That is the main reason to register rather than take a copy of the file.

What we do with your details

Your email address, and anything else you choose to enter, is held in our mailing-list account at MailerLite and used for two things: sending you the files, and telling you when the research changes. We do not sell the list, and we do not pass it to anyone else. Every message carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes you from the list. The privacy notice sets out the detail, including how to ask what we hold and have it removed.

Registration is not a commercial engagement and creates no obligation on either side. If you would rather not be on a list at all, the update feed carries the same notices with no registration and no email address.
