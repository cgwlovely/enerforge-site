Case 01 · Northern Territory · public-data screen

Remote Mining Asset — Hybrid Energy Pre-DD

A public-information desktop Pre-DD screen of a remote gold-mine PV + BESS + diesel hybrid, covering F1 Approval, F2 Site, F3 Microgrid and F4 Financeability. Published as a method demonstration; the asset is not named and was not involved in the work.

Read the main case report → How the method works

Project snapshot

Remote mine hybrid-energy screen

Location
Northern Territory
Technology
Solar PV + BESS + diesel microgrid
Review type
Independent public-information Pre-DD

Key question

Which hybrid configuration is credible enough to advance to owner data, hourly dispatch, EPC pricing and specialist due diligence?

Key findings

  • S4, an indicative 60% annual-renewable pathway, was selected for further validation rather than adopted as a final design.
  • The PV and BESS areas identified in the siting screen were preliminary desktop search areas, not selected sites or approved equipment footprints. The site-specific map outputs are not published.
  • A modelled 1.30x DSCR constraint is a debt-sizing assumption, not lender approval.
  • Owner data, hourly dispatch, equipment sizing and specialist review remain required.
