Projects and applied research

Project screens take a single site and ask whether it can advance. Market reconstructions take a whole market and ask how big it is and who controls it. Both are built from public records and published with their evidence grades.

How to read these

These are public-information desktop screens of the F1-F4 Pre-DD method, not commissioned advice, lender due diligence or bank-approved models. Figures and zones are indicative early-stage outputs. Nothing here is legal, financial, tax, engineering or investment advice.

The largest mine-electrification loads may be invisible in today’s electricity data

National reconciliation of the diesel and generation accounts, showing that mining’s 299.6 PJ of diesel is mostly mobile equipment; converted to electricity it is a theoretical 3.9–4.65 GW of average load, an upper bound that current electricity data cannot see.

What you receive

What a project screen delivers

One screen produces three documents, written for different readers at different stages of the conversation.

Output 01

Integrated Pre-DD Report

For public websites, first conversations and non-confidential sharing.

  • Project context
  • F1-F4 readiness snapshot
  • Preliminary siting opportunity and constraint screen
  • Key constraints
  • Limitations
  • Recommended next step
Output 02

Owner Pre-DD Report

For first owner / sponsor discussions.

  • F1 ten-question approval delta screen
  • F2 candidate site and constraint map
  • F3 microgrid boundary questions
  • F4 commercial and financeability logic
  • Integrated gap register
  • Owner data request
  • First meeting agenda
Output 03

Due-Diligence Question Register

For turning uncertainty into action.

  • Questions that remain open
  • Why each question matters
  • Who should answer it
  • Evidence required, and when
  • Conditions precedent
  • Project milestone: financial close, notice to proceed or commercial operation
