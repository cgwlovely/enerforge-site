Case studies

Worked examples, at two scales

Project screens take a single site and ask whether it can advance — approval delta, site suitability, microgrid boundary, financeability readiness. Sector studies take a whole market and ask how big it actually is and who controls it, by reconciling site-level evidence against national accounts. Same engine, two scales.

How to read these

These are public-information desktop screens of the F1-F4 Pre-DD method, not commissioned advice, lender due diligence or bank-approved models. Figures and zones are indicative early-stage outputs. Nothing here is legal, financial, tax, engineering or investment advice.

HeliovulcanEnergy Advisors

Heliovulcan is an independent industrial intelligence practice. It reconstructs Australian industrial assets and energy projects from public records and agreed inputs — energy, emissions, spatial, ownership and commercial context — reconciles them against independently built accounts, and grades the evidence behind every figure. Outputs include asset profiles, project screens, the F1–F4 Pre-DD review and sector analysis.
