Consultations · Method

How submissions are prepared

A submission is only useful to a reader who can trace it back to evidence. This page sets out which consultations we answer, what each published record contains, and the discipline every figure is held to.

Which consultations

The process must be asking a question the public record can answer

The test is narrow and deliberately so. In scope: which facilities or sectors are exposed to a proposed rule change, and by how much; whether a stated national or sectoral quantity reconciles bottom-up; where a proposed mechanism would and would not reach in practice; what the public record can and cannot establish about an industrial population.

Out of scope: legal interpretation of instruments or drafting; positions advocating a commercial outcome for a client; matters requiring confidential operator data to answer honestly; anything where the honest answer is that the evidence is not there.

What each record contains

The question examined, the evidence used, the findings, the recommendations and what remains unresolved

Every published submission page carries those five parts in that order, followed by sources, the PDF and related research. Any part that cannot be completed honestly is shown as unresolved rather than omitted. The institution is named exactly as it names itself, and the question addressed is the specific one from the consultation paper, not the whole paper.

Evidence discipline

Every figure carries the label it was established at

Regulatory filing, licence record, company disclosure or calculated. Anything public data cannot settle is named rather than estimated. Where a published figure cannot be reproduced, the difference is stated in the submission. The labels are set out in full on the methods page.

Bodies monitored

A small number of bodies run the relevant processes

Climate Change Authority · Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water · Australian Energy Market Operator · Australian Energy Regulator · Australian Energy Market Commission · Parliamentary committees. They are listed because they run public consultation processes. Heliovulcan is not appointed, accredited or endorsed by any of them, and does not provide legal advice, regulatory representation or lobbying services. Submissions are independent analytical contributions on its own behalf, not on behalf of any client.
