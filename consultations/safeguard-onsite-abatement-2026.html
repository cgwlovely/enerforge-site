Onsite abatement under the Safeguard Mechanism: what the public registers can and cannot show

Deployment at one site is a screening signal, not evidence that a technology is cost-effective across a subsector. The registers show where abatement projects sit; they do not show who controls the asset or what the abatement costs.

The question

Question 10 of the 2026 Annual Progress Advice consultation: whether the Safeguard Mechanism should play a stronger role in encouraging onsite abatement relative to ACCU flexibility, what objectives should guide that assessment, and whether additional incentives or constraints are warranted. Public-register evidence is also provided against Question 9.

Key findings

  1. Onsite abatement projects cluster in one technology and one window.

    Six projects were registered after the reformed baselines commenced. All are coal mine waste gas electricity generation; four were registered within four days of each other in March 2025.

  2. Deployment at one site is not evidence of cost-effectiveness across a subsector.

    It is a screening signal. Whether the same measure is available to the next facility depends on who controls the plant and what it costs there.

  3. Asset control and marginal abatement cost cannot be established from public registers.

    This is why the submission recommends a two-tier assessment rather than a limit set by industry classification.

Recommendation

Two-tier assessmentAssess the case for stronger onsite-abatement incentives at the level of asset control and abatement cost, not by industry classification, and require the information the public registers cannot supply from the facilities themselves.

Open questions

The submission identifies 24 facilities surrendering ACCUs at or above 30% of baseline, where the Authority and Department report 23. The difference may lie in the treatment of deemed surrender; nothing in the findings rests on it.

Sources

CER Safeguard facility reported emissions, FY2024–25 and FY2023–24 (regulatory filing). CER ACCU Scheme Project Register, 31 May 2026 (regulatory filing).
