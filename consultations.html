Submission · September 2026 · DCCEEW, 2026-27 Safeguard Mechanism Review

The post-2030 decline rate and the trade-exposed pathway have to be calibrated together

A mechanical calibration from the published facility register gives a starting point of 3.53% a year for the 62% target and 5.99% for the 70% target under current rules. Every combination of default and trade-exposed rates that meets a target lies on one iso-target curve; the policy decision is the choice of a point on it.

Submission · August 2026 · Senate inquiry

Data centres: regulatory records are not connected at the facility level

Australia regulates different aspects of data centres through energy, connection, planning, environmental, water and rating systems. The public records reviewed use different identifiers and boundaries, with no crosswalk connecting them to the same site.
