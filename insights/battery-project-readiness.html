What makes a BESS project development-ready?

A battery project is ready when its site control, planning, connection, revenue, delivery structure and financing hold together, and when the interfaces between them are owned. These five notes take the readiness question one layer at a time. They are frameworks and practitioner judgement, drawn from cited industry sources and Heliovulcan’s screening work, not a representative sample of Australian projects.

Framework

Path to financial close

Financial close is the convergence of rights, contracts, risk allocation and evidence. Each item on the path is a condition to close, not a modelling step; the five notes above address the conditions that most often remain open.
