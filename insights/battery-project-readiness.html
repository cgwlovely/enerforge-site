What makes a BESS project development-ready?

A battery project is ready when its site control, planning approval, grid connection, revenue, delivery structure and financing are all in place, and when responsibility for every interface between them is clear. Here, development-ready means that the project has no unresolved issue likely to stop construction or financing. These five notes take the question one layer at a time. The series combines published industry sources with lessons from our screening work. It is not a representative sample of Australian projects.

The series

Read the series in order or start with the unresolved issue

  • 01 · Connection queue

    A BESS connection queue is not a construction pipeline

    A connection queue shows developer interest, not construction-ready capacity. Treat a project as credible only when it has land, planning approval, meaningful connection progress, a delivery plan and a financing route.

  • 02 · Brownfield rights

    Existing grid rights can improve a brownfield battery project

    Existing substations, lines, permits and injection rights can save time and reduce uncertainty, provided their remaining life, capacity, protection and shared-use limits still suit the project.

    Read →
  • 03 · Development risk before capital

    For early-stage batteries, project readiness may matter more than available capital

    Where capital is available, funding decisions turn on resolved land, approvals, connection, contracts and cash flow. A delay can increase costs, postpone revenue and weaken the financing case at the same time.

    Read →
  • 04 · Hybrid interfaces

    Review hybrid assets as one commercial and operational system

    Shared connections, dispatch rights and contracts create risks that separate solar and battery reviews miss. Every interface needs a named responsible party.

    A battery is ready to build only when its key rights, approvals and contracts are complete

    A completeness test across seven items. A project can gain substantial value when its final critical gaps are resolved.
