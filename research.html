Research & insights

Open research on Australian industry, energy and investment

Published work built the same way as everything else here: from the public record, with every figure carrying its evidence grade and the unresolved questions named rather than filled in. Several pieces reach a negative conclusion — that a market is smaller than it looks, or that a route does not exist. Those are published for the same reason as the rest.

What these are

Analytical research notes, not formal academic publications and not investment advice. They are written to help owners, developers, investors and policy readers think more clearly about decisions that turn on evidence. Where research has been submitted to a public consultation, it is recorded on the consultations page.

HeliovulcanEnergy Advisors

Heliovulcan is an independent industrial intelligence practice. It reconstructs Australian industrial assets and energy projects from public records and agreed inputs — energy, emissions, spatial, ownership and commercial context — reconciles them against independently built accounts, and grades the evidence behind every figure. Outputs include research, asset profiles, project screens, the F1–F4 Pre-DD review and sector analysis.
