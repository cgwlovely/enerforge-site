Interactive model

The post-2030 decline rate cannot be set in isolation

Question
What post-2030 decline rate would meet the 2035 target? How would that rate change under different assumptions for trade-exposed facilities, production, closures and new projects?
Finding
On the starting assumptions, the 62% target requires a decline rate of 3.53 percentage points a year, compared with the legislated indicative rate of 3.285. Different combinations of the default and trade-exposed rates reach the same target.
Data
FY2024-25 Safeguard register, 208 facilities on 209 rows, 16 financial years; 135 published figures recomputed in the browser

The largest future mine-electrification loads may not appear in current electricity data

Question
How much electrical load could mine-fleet electrification create, and which sites would a screen based on current electricity use miss?
Finding
Mining reported 299.6 PJ of diesel, most of it used by mobile equipment. Converted to electricity that is a theoretical 3.9–4.65 GW of average load. It is an upper bound, not a market forecast.
Data
Australian Energy Statistics 2023-24; NGER and NPI registers; Heliovulcan’s 370-site mine screen

A lower Safeguard threshold would also tighten baselines for existing facilities

Question
How would a lower coverage threshold affect facilities already inside the Safeguard Mechanism?
Finding
24 covered facilities sit on the 100,000 t minimum baseline today and 45 are projected to by FY2029-30; a 75,000 t threshold would remove at least 0.92 Mt a year of baseline from facilities already covered, an effect the consultation paper does not quantify.
Data
FY2023-24 and FY2024-25 registers; Safeguard Rule ss 10(1), 11, 56(4) and 58B; consultation paper table 3.3
August 2026 · 11-minute read Project-register analysis

Public funding appears mainly in first-of-a-kind mine-energy projects

Question
Which Australian mine microgrid and battery projects received publicly disclosed funding, and what does that pattern suggest for a funding case?
Finding
We found a public funding record for 3 of the 23 operating projects, each described by its funder as a first application in Australia. We found no public record for the other 20, which does not prove they received no public funding.
Data
Heliovulcan register of 23 operating mine microgrid and hybrid BESS projects; ARENA, CEFC and state program records as at 3 August 2026
