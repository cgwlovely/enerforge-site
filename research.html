Interactive model

The post-2030 decline rate cannot be set in isolation

Question
What post-2030 decline rate meets the 2035 target, and how the trade-exposed pathway, production, closures and new entrants trade against it?
Finding
3.53 pp/yr required for a 62% target on the starting-point assumptions, against a statutory 3.285; every combination that meets the target lies on one iso-target curve.
Data
FY2024-25 Safeguard register, 208 facilities on 209 rows, 16 financial years; 135 published figures recomputed in the browser

The largest future mine-electrification loads may not appear in current electricity data

Question
How much electrical load could mining-fleet electrification create, and which sites would a current-load screen miss?
Finding
Reported mining diesel of 299.6 PJ is mostly mobile use; converted to electricity it is a theoretical 3.9–4.65 GW of average load, an upper bound, not a market forecast.
Data
Australian Energy Statistics 2023-24; NGER and NPI registers; Heliovulcan’s 370-site mine screen
August 2026 · 12-minute read Quantitative policy analysis

Lowering the Safeguard threshold also tightens baselines for facilities already covered

Question
What changes when the Safeguard coverage threshold is lowered?
Finding
24 covered facilities sit on the 100,000 t floor today and 45 by 2029-30; a 75,000 t threshold withdraws about 0.92 Mt a year of baseline from facilities already in the scheme, an effect absent from the consultation table.
Data
FY2023-24 and FY2024-25 registers; Safeguard Rule ss 10(1), 11, 56(4), 57; consultation paper table 3.3
August 2026 · 15-minute read Project-register analysis

Public funding is concentrated in first-of-a-kind mine-energy projects

Question
Which Australian mine microgrid and BESS projects carried public funding, and what does the pattern imply for a funding case?
Finding
A public funding record was identified for 3 of 23 operating projects, all first-of-a-kind; the other 20 have no record found, which is not treated as proof that none was received.
Data
Heliovulcan register of 23 operating mine microgrid and hybrid BESS projects; ARENA, CEFC and state program records as at 3 August 2026
