The largest future mine-electrification loads may not appear in current electricity data

3.9–4.65 GW

Most reported mining diesel is used in mobile equipment and therefore does not appear as electricity demand. Its electrical equivalent is an upper bound of 3.9–4.65 GW; the addressable market is smaller and depends on fleet timing, mine life, power supply and fuel tax credits.
