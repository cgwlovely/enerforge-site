Can a remote Northern Territory gold mine replace most of its diesel with solar and storage?

A public-information Pre-DD screen of a hybrid PV, battery and diesel pathway: approvals, site, microgrid boundary and financeability, run before any development cost is committed. Scenario S4, a 60% renewable hybrid, was selected for hourly modelling and commercial validation.

F1Approval delta: what the existing consents cover and what a hybrid would add
F2Site: candidate zones and the constraints that rule them out
F3Microgrid boundary: what the battery must carry when the diesel steps back
F4Financeability: whether the cashflow a lender can sign survives the customer's savings
